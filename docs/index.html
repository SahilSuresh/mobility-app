Restore Premium on a new phone

Open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Premium follows the Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

Cancel or change your subscription

On iPhone: Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions. On Android: the Play Store app, then your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Subscribers can also tap Manage subscription in the app's Settings. To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com or through Google Play.

Your data
